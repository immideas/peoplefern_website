Your People. Your Processes. One Platform.

PeopleVault was built by a team that has seen firsthand how much time HR teams lose to fragmented records, manual processes and disconnected tools.

We set out to build a platform that brings the essentials of workforce management — employee data, attendance, leave, payroll and policies — into one dependable system.

Why PeopleVault?

HR teams can spend more time managing spreadsheets than managing people. Fragmented employee data, manual attendance tracking and disconnected leave and payroll processes can create errors, slow decisions and frustrate both HR and employees.

PeopleVault is designed to solve that with one platform and one centralized source of truth for workforce operations.
